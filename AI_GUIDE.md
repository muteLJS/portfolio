# AI GUIDE

## Project

This is a one-page portfolio website for a UI/UX designer.

The portfolio is based on a quiet nature-driven story:

water droplet → quiet lake → ripple → branch → project leaves → hill and rainbow.

The website must still work as a real portfolio.
Readability, hierarchy, and usability are more important than visual effects.

## Core Concept

A single water droplet falls into a quiet lake.
The ripple slowly expands.
That small beginning becomes the visual metaphor of the portfolio.

Main message:

"Moving forward, one step at a time."

Korean title copy:

앞으로 갑니다, 한 걸음씩

## Important Direction

The experience should feel:

- quiet
- calm
- realistic
- atmospheric
- emotional but restrained
- immersive
- elegant
- minimal
- readable

## Do Not

Do not create:

- neon UI
- futuristic cyberpunk visuals
- flashy animation
- game-like fantasy scenes
- exaggerated cinematic effects
- excessive particles
- random gradients
- generic AI template feeling
- aggressive scroll effects
- unreadable text layouts

## Work Rule

Do not redesign the whole portfolio without request.

Work section by section:

1. Hero
2. About
3. Projects
4. Contact

Preserve the concept and visual tone across all sections.
