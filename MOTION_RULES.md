# MOTION RULES

## Motion Philosophy

Motion should feel environmental, not interface-heavy.

Animations should feel like:

- fog drifting
- water slowly moving
- ripples expanding
- branches gently appearing
- leaves moving softly

## Hero Motion

Hero uses a quiet lake video as the main background.

The camera must feel static.
Do not add artificial camera movement.

The video should show:

- quiet lake
- dawn atmosphere
- subtle fog
- soft forest
- water droplet falling
- ripple expanding
- final subtle ripple remaining

## Scroll Motion

Use smooth section transitions.

Avoid aggressive scroll hijacking.
Avoid fast snapping that feels uncomfortable.

Scroll should feel calm and guided.

## Ripple Motion

Ripples should be:

- slow
- circular
- layered
- soft
- long fading
- subtle

The ripple represents the beginning of the portfolio story.

## Hover Motion

Hover effects should be subtle.

Allowed:

- soft opacity change
- slight translate
- gentle blur
- thin line reveal

Avoid:

- strong bounce
- scale too much
- spinning
- glowing neon

## Transition Direction

Hero → About:
ripple expands and the story opens.

About → Projects:
natural branch or leaf structure begins.

Projects:
project cards can be represented as leaves connected to branches.

Contact:
hill and rainbow scene, quiet ending.
