# PROMPTS

## Hero Lake Video Prompt

Ultra realistic quiet lake at dawn.

The camera is completely static and never moves.

A calm dark lake is surrounded by distant natural forest in a soft curved shape around the water.
The trees feel real and detailed, with natural depth and subtle variation.
Large open empty space remains above the lake and sky.

The atmosphere feels cold, quiet, isolated, and natural.

Very subtle fog slowly drifts above the water surface.
Soft clouds move slowly across the sky.
Very gentle wind slightly moves distant tree branches.

The water surface is almost completely still with only extremely subtle natural movement.

At first, nothing happens.

After a few quiet seconds,
a single large transparent realistic water droplet slowly appears from high above the frame and falls naturally into the exact center of the lake.

The droplet is noticeably larger than normal rain,
but still physically believable and natural.

The droplet feels heavy, soft, clear, realistic, and slightly reflective.

When the droplet touches the water,
there is almost no splash.

Instead,
soft realistic ripples slowly spread outward across the lake surface in smooth expanding circular waves.

The ripples continue expanding gently across the water in sequence,
creating calm layered wave patterns.

The final state should retain subtle continuous ripples on the lake surface,
as if the impact still quietly remains.

No dramatic splash.
No explosion effect.
No cinematic trailer feeling.
No fantasy atmosphere.
No exaggerated lighting.
No camera shake.
No zoom.
No depth of field blur.
No particles.
No fast movement.
No stylized animation.

Everything should feel like real nature captured by a stationary camera.

Minimal environmental motion only.

The emotional tone should feel:
quiet,
calm,
restrained,
immersive,
real,
lonely,
and atmospheric.

## Recommended Video Settings

- Ratio: 16:9
- Duration: 6 to 8 seconds
- Camera motion: none
- Style: ultra realistic
- Motion: minimal
- Loop: edit later if needed

## ChatGPT Image Prompts for Video Sources

Use these prompts to generate still source images first. The generated images should then be used as the base images for Adobe Firefly image-to-video generation.

General image requirements:

- 16:9 landscape composition
- Ultra realistic photographic style
- Static-camera friendly composition
- No text, no UI, no logo, no watermark
- Leave enough negative space for portfolio content
- Natural lighting and restrained colors
- Avoid strong foreground objects unless specified

### Hero Source Image

Purpose:
Base image for the hero intro and hero loop videos.

Prompt:

Create an ultra realistic wide landscape photograph of a calm lake at early dawn, matching a clean horizontal composition. The camera is completely static, low and front-facing across the water. A low distant forest line runs along the far shore on the left and right, leaving a broad open sky above and a wide mirror-like lake surface below. The scene is airy, quiet, and spacious rather than dark or enclosed. The sky is pale blue-gray with soft layered clouds and a faint warm peach glow near the horizon. A thin horizontal band of mist floats just above the far waterline. The lake reflects the clouds and forest softly, with very subtle natural texture on the water. Keep the center of the lake open and uncluttered for a later droplet impact and ripple animation. No text, no typography, no scroll indicator, no UI elements, no people, no buildings, no boats.

Negative prompt:

Text, letters, captions, scroll text, UI overlay, logo, watermark, people, buildings, boats, dark enclosed forest, narrow lake valley, heavy shadows, dramatic lighting, artificial glow, heavy mist, strong waves, crowded trees, high tree walls, cartoon style, illustration style, surreal colors.

Recommended Firefly motion from this image:

Add very subtle fog drift, soft cloud movement, a single realistic transparent water droplet falling into the exact center of the lake, and slow circular ripples spreading outward with almost no splash.
